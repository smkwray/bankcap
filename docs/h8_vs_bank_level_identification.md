# H.8 versus bank-level identification

## What H.8 can do

H.8 can show aggregate bank-group balance-sheet movement. It is useful for asking whether broad bank
categories appear to respond differently around Treasury financing and liquidity-plumbing regimes.
It is inexpensive, transparent, and suitable for an early mechanism screen.

## What H.8 cannot do

H.8 cannot identify individual-bank absorption or heterogeneity. The public groups are aggregates, and
securities categories may combine Treasuries and agency securities. H.8 also cannot solve merger,
survivorship, or bank-level pre-trend issues.

## What bank-level data would add

Call Reports or FR Y-9C would allow:

- stable bank or holding-company identifiers;
- pre-period liquidity, duration, securities, loan, and deposit characteristics;
- SLR/capital/risk-weight proxies where reported;
- merger and survivorship handling;
- fixed effects and pre-trend checks;
- heterogeneity designs based on pre-period capacity measures.

## Why not start there?

Bank-level ingestion is expensive. The H.8 screen is a gate: if the bank-group mechanism does not show
stable and interpretable patterns, heavy Call Report or FR Y-9C engineering is unlikely to pay off.
