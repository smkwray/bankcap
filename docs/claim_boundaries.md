# Claim boundaries

These are hard boundaries for `bankcap` until bank-level data are explicitly added.

## Allowed H.8 claims

- H.8 bank-group trends provide mechanism context.
- Large domestic, small domestic, and foreign-related groups can be compared descriptively.
- H.8 securities, deposits, loans, and cash assets can be screened around Treasury financing regimes.
- Bill-heavy versus coupon-heavy contexts can be compared as descriptive co-movement.

## Not allowed from H.8 alone

- No bank-level heterogeneity claims.
- No claim that a specific bank absorbed Treasury supply.
- No causal claim that issuance composition caused deposits, loans, cash, or securities to move.
- No Treasury-only securities claim unless the source mapping identifies Treasury securities separately.
- No SLR-constrained-bank identification without bank-level capital, exposure, and balance-sheet data.

## Required wording

Use labels such as:

- “H.8 bank-group mechanism screen”;
- “H.8 securities aggregate, potentially Treasury plus agency securities”;
- “descriptive association”;
- “go/no-go evidence for a bank-level extension.”

Avoid labels such as:

- “bank-level absorption result”;
- “causal crowd-out”;
- “SLR-constrained banks absorbed more Treasuries”;
- “Treasury securities holdings” if the measure is H.8 securities aggregate.
